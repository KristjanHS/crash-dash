Email alerts

Band alerts by email - and your account

Get a mail when the alert band moves (OK / WATCH / WARNING / CRITICAL) - a few a month. Subscribing also creates your crash-dash account: the confirmation link you click signs this tab in to the full dashboard, the lab, exposure and a private holdings journal that only you can read. Already have an account? Sign in instead - no need to subscribe again.

Both subscribing and unsubscribing are confirmed by a link sent to the address, so nobody can sign you up or remove you but you. Unsubscribing stops the mails only - your account and journal stay, and you can still sign in.
