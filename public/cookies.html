Cookie policy

This site sets no cookies

crash-dash does not set any cookies. It runs no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers, so there are no tracking or profiling cookies to consent to. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing here that ePrivacy Art. 5(3) requires prior consent for.

What it does store, on your device

The site uses your browser's local storage and session storage - not cookies, and never sent to a server for tracking - strictly to make the pages work:

  • View preferences (local storage): remembers your dashboard and lab choices, such as which mode or panels are shown, so the page opens the way you left it.
  • Journal sign-in (local and session storage), only if you use the optional journal page: your Supabase connection details are kept in local storage and your sign-in token in session storage, on your own device, so you stay signed in. See the Privacy policy.

You can clear any of this at any time through your browser's site-data controls; doing so only resets your preferences and signs you out of the journal.
