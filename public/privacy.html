Privacy policy

Information under Arts. 13-14 GDPR. The controller is the operator named in the Imprint.

What the public pages collect

The dashboard, exposure and lab pages have no accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers. They set no cookies. Small view preferences (for example which panels or mode are shown) are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device and are never sent to a server - see the Cookie policy.

Server logs

The site is hosted on Vercel and its data store on Supabase. As part of delivering the pages and the read API, the hosting infrastructure processes technical request data such as IP address, timestamp and requested URL in server and security logs. The legal basis is the operator's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in operating and securing the service. These providers act as processors and may store or process data outside the EU/EEA under the relevant transfer safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

The optional holdings journal

The journal page is optional and self-hosted: you connect it to your own Supabase project. Your email is used only to send a sign-in magic link, and your entries are stored in the database you connect. Your connection details stay in your browser's local storage and your sign-in token in session storage on your own device.

Recipients

Hosting and infrastructure providers (Vercel, Supabase) as processors. The push channel (ntfy.sh) is used only where you subscribe to it. No personal data is sold or shared for advertising.

Retention

Server and security logs are kept only as long as the hosting providers retain them for operational and security purposes.

Erasing your account (see Your rights) removes your journal entries, your email subscription and the sign-in account itself. It does not touch the market-data series, the alert history or the calibration record - none of those hold anything about you. Two records outlive the account by design: the sign-in provider's own security audit log, and the processor-side logs of the mail sender (Brevo) and the host (Vercel), each kept for their own retention period.

Your rights

Subject to the GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and object to processing (Arts. 15-21). You may also lodge a complaint with the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, AKI).

There are two routes to erasure. If you can sign in, do it yourself: Holdings journal carries a Delete my account control that removes your journal entries, your email subscription and the sign-in account in one step, immediately and without asking us. If you cannot sign in - a lost address, a link that no longer arrives - contact the operator at the email address in the Imprint and ask; we do the same thing by hand. Unsubscribing from the emails is a separate, smaller thing: it stops the mails and keeps the account.
