What this page is, and how to read it

It notifies, nothing more: it holds no money, places no orders and knows nothing about you. Not investment advice.

New here? The page reads top to bottom, from the verdict down to the raw material. Floors shows which channels lifted today's band above what the danger score alone earned. The families board is the signal side - the six families each showing their worst indicator, once that reading has held long enough to count. The danger ledger ranks the nine rungs of the risk ladder by their own danger, most dangerous first, so you can see why the held rung is the one held. Indicator detail plots one indicator against the cut-points that turn it Green, Amber or Red; licensed market feeds show their state only.

Everything here is read-only and needs no account - nothing you click changes the alert or the position. Longer explanation: How it works. Going deeper: the Lab (every method and cut, on live data), the Journal (what the gauge actually published, over time) and Exposure (a posture dial off the same signals) - each needs a sign-in.
