Pattern: **the supply-shock top signature is half-formed.** Oil is back above $100 with copper and breakevens

expectations roll first, oil last). What is missing is the roll in oil itself and the dollar surge.

## 2. What turns this into a bear with no recovery inside a month

Any TWO of the following. One alone (2011: copper-first, oil held) gave a −15…−20% drift over quarters; two
(2008, 2014, 2022: oil roll + dollar surge) gave −40…−75% with no recovery inside a year. The 2026 shock is
Hormuz-driven, so the unwind is a **binary supply event**, not a slow demand roll.

alone (trigger 1) costs ~−25…−30% on the energy sleeve inside weeks (2022 pace), before any metals leg.
